THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I’m GEDASXH.

I’m Hilary Cyprian Hyera, a graphic designer and IT enthusiast who enjoys combining visual creativity with practical technical skills.

My goal is simple: understand what a person or business needs, then help deliver a solution that looks professional and works well. That could mean a strong promotional design, help with a computer, a network setup or a website.

This portfolio is a growing collection of my work and learning. I believe in improving with every project, communicating clearly and treating every client’s idea with care.
